This is the sole authoritative Day 6 timetable. Durations remain provisional until piloted with a beginner.

到達目標：LLM・支援ツール・エージェント・ハーネスを区別し、現場の問題を要件にし、実装前に計画を求め、範囲を制限し、受入基準で結果を確かめ、引き継げること。**コードはAIが書けても、問題を定めて結果を確かめる責任は人にあります。**

Outcome: distinguish models, assistants, agents, harnesses and tools; turn a factory problem into requirements; request a plan before implementation; limit scope; verify results against acceptance criteria; and hand over the work. **An agent may write code, but the human defines the problem and verifies the results.**
